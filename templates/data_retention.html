Workspace data retention policy

To keep storage costs predictable, DataViz Dash retains standard workspace content for {{ retention_days }} days from the date it is created.

Automatic deletion after {{ retention_days }} days

Uploaded datasets, stored rows, cleaned working copies, sample datasets, and saved dashboards are permanently deleted when their individual retention period ends.

01

What is deleted

  • Uploaded CSV, XLS, and XLSX dataset content stored in the workspace.
  • Derived rows and cleaned working copies created from those uploads.
  • Sample datasets created through the one-click demo.
  • Saved dashboards, pages, charts, layouts, and filters.

Editing, cleaning, viewing, or signing in does not restart the retention period. The deadline is based on the original creation date of each dataset or dashboard.

02

Before your data expires

Download cleaned datasets and export dashboard output before the displayed expiry date. Automatic deletion is permanent and deleted workspace content cannot be restored through DataViz Dash.

03

Extended Storage add-on

If you need workspace data beyond {{ retention_days }} days, apply for the separate Extended Storage add-on before the data expires.

  • Designed only for longer workspace-data retention.
  • Purchased separately from Free, Pro, Business, or Agency access.
  • Retention period and storage capacity are agreed for the account.
  • Pricing is provided as a custom quote until self-serve billing is available.
View storage option
04

Records kept separately

Account identity, authentication, security, fraud-prevention, billing, and legally required operational records are not workspace content. They may be retained as needed to operate the account, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.
